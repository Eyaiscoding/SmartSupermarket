## 🎯 What This System Does (Elevator Pitch)

Imagine a smart supermarket that acts like a mini power plant. It generates solar energy, stores it in batteries, and intelligently decides when to use grid power, battery power, or even sell excess energy to neighbors. **The key rule:** refrigeration (frozen food) NEVER loses power, even if we have to turn off the lights.

This dashboard shows how well the system is managing energy in real-time, protecting critical equipment, and saving money.
